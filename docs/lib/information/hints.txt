Strength affects how hard you hit, as well as how heavy your load will be.
Agility affects your armor class and how often you dodge attacks.
Dexterity affects how often you parry attacks and affects how many items you can carry at once.
Wisdom determines how quickly Priest classes pray for spells.
Intelligence affects how easily you can search for hidden items or exits and affects how quickly Magic-User classes memorize spells.
Charisma affects the prices stores charge you and affects how obedient your summoned monsters (pets) are.
Save money and buy a boat!  There's a whole world out there waiting for you! Use help ship to learn how.
Some magic works at a distance - try "cast 'spellname' <person> <direction>" to hit someone in the next room!
"Luring" means hitting a mob and running away to make it follow you.  Luring is useful to trick a mob into leaving its friends, so you can kill it more easily.
Experiment with different social commands. Some are useful in actual game play. Example: "beckon" asks someone to follow and consent to you, or "moving" announces that you are moving ahead.
Killing other players and taking their equipment is completely legal, but so is retribution!  Beware of your victims' allies.

New to Duris? Type "rules" and read them carefully. Not knowing a rule does not excuse breaking it.
Ready to log off? Mortals cannot simply quit, so "rent" at an inn or "camp" in a safe spot to leave with your gear.
Stay logged off for at least 9 real hours to return with a rested experience bonus. After 20 hours you will be well-rested, which is even better.
Carrying lots of coins? Visit a bank and use "deposit", "withdraw" and "balance". Coins on you can be stolen, or lost when you die.
After you die, your gear stays in your corpse. Hurry back and type "get all corpse" before it decays, about two hours later.
Deaths below level 30 cost no experience. From level 30 on, dying can cost you experience and even a level.
Several targets with the same name? Use a number, such as "kill 2.goblin" or "get 2.sword". Typing "get all.sword" takes every match.

The Gods of Duris grant automatic bonuses for limited times. Type "boon" to see which ones are active.

Type "leaderboard" to see the top 20 characters in the game. Their scores come from levels, experience, player kills, ship reputation and epic points. Type "leaderboard quests" to see who has completed the most quests.
Wearing several pieces of random gear from the same zone forms a named set, which can give you extra hit points and spells. Type "namedreport" to see which spells each set gives and how many pieces you need.
Type "quest" to see your bartender quest, including its target, area and progress.
Bartender quests ask you to kill monsters or speak to someone. Finishing one earns you experience, coins and an item.
Can't find the target of your bartender quest? Type "ask bartender map" to buy a map, then type "quest" to see it.
Want to do a bartender quest with friends? Have each friend "consent" you, then type "quest share <player>" to share it with them. You can share a quest with up to four friends.
Mobs that offer quests show a (Q) beside their names. Type "toggle quest" to turn this marker on or off.
As you earn epic points, the Gods may give you an epic task, such as finding a runestone. Type "score" to see your current task, because some rewards are reduced until you finish it.

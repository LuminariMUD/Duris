COLUMN NOTES
WT = stored full item weight in pounds. Negative weights are real template values. A zero/one-pound item contributes 0 to the per-item half-weight attack tally, but a one-pound item still adds full weight for monk AC/damage.
armor = stored armor value[0], not computed character AC. Material, other armor rules, modifiers, and monk encumbrance still apply.
HITROLL/DAMROLL are accuracy/damage bonuses; HP is hit points; MV is movement. _MAX increases that stat ceiling.
Negative saving modifiers improve saves. Negative COMBAT_PULSE means a combat-speed modifier, not an extra attack count.
Dropper L is the NPC level, NOT a player equip level, solo recommendation, or progression gate. Shops are identified independently from keeper levels.
Prototype MAGIC is ITEM2_MAGIC. Below level 51, nonmagic gloves do not bypass innate weapon immunity on an unarmed hit.
Proc assignment reports native specs.assign.c and embedded _proclib_ evidence; generic weapon packed-spell slots are not glove-on-hit support.

RELEVANT MONK RULES
Per item equip gate: weight <= floor(current STR / 10) by checked-in property. Back-slot admission exception does not exempt gloves.
AC uses full equipped weight W: monk bonus clamp(-400, -4*level - martial_arts + 3*max(0,W-floor((60-level)/6)), 50).
Bare-hand damage loses full equipped weight above floor(level*STR/100), with a floor of 1. Damroll is applied later.
Base attacks use E=sum(trunc(weight_i/2)); lose one base attack per full 10 pounds above floor(STR/2), retaining at least one. Loose inventory is excluded.
Weapons, shields, held items and worn lights interfere with normal monk attack handling. Wearing gloves is a separate slot and does not itself occupy those hand-item slots.
Normal newbie monk kit gives 1147-1151 and no gloves. Chaos standard/enhanceable monk main-hands are both 5817; Illusionist also gets 5817.
5817 quest consumes 5813 (-10 WT), five huge blood crystals 500025, and 400 platinum. The result weighs +4: a 14-pound increase that can hurt monk AC/base damage.
For class-wide balancing, gate shared-item procs on Monk eligibility in the proc itself. Nearly all candidates are shared with other classes.
The melee hit path calls weapon_proc only when an actual weapon exists. A new weapon-style on-hit proc on glove value[5..7] will not automatically trigger for unarmed monks.

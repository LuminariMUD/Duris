Why a value changed

Every change comes from one of five corrections. Each row in the list names the one that applies.

  • Stat order117 items · all up

    Two items with the same stats could score differently depending on the order the stats were stored in. Order no longer matters.

  • Split stats13 items · 9 up, 4 down

    A stat listed twice, such as STR +3 and STR +3, scored lower than a single STR +6. The total is now valued once.

  • Spell procs13 items · all up

    Items that fire one of several spells at random lost a point or more to early rounding. The fraction is now kept until the end.

  • Zero stats4 items · 1 up, 3 down

    A stat slot holding +0 shifted the value even though it grants nothing. It now counts for nothing.

  • Inactive proc1 item · down

    The claymore of Zarthos was credited for a spell it cannot cast. With that credit removed it drops from 338 to 111, and can be forged from level 37.

Randomly generated gear

Random drops have no fixed entry to list, so we tested 8,720 generated samples instead. About 6% of them changed, split evenly: 269 went up and 258 went down, typically by around 10 points.

A random item is affected only if it mixes flat and percentage stats, repeats a stat, or carries a +0 stat. Lore or identify it to see its current value.

What follows the number

Item value sets the level needed to craft or enhance an item, the materials a recipe uses, the grade of salvage, and which faerie bag an item can drop from. For most of this list a few points changes none of that. Where a value crosses a threshold, the requirement moves with it: for 64 items the level needed to craft or enhance them shifts, 52 of them by a single level.
